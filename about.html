Who we are

A shared learning space for clearer medical understanding.

Founded on 20 November 2024 by Dr. dr. Bob Andinata, Sp.B., Subsp. Onk(K), BA Medicale began as a space for learning and knowledge-sharing in medicine and health.

Its purpose is to connect understandable public education with deeper professional learning. Initial editorial depth comes from oncology, neoplasia, tumor, and cancer, while the platform continues to develop education across wider medical disciplines.

Our vision

A trusted and inclusive medical-learning platform.

BA Medicale aims to help the public, doctors, and healthcare workers learn, share, and grow through high-quality medical education.

Our mission

Make quality learning more useful and more reachable.

  • Provide accurate, relevant, source-aware medical and health education.
  • Support professional development through practical learning and training.
  • Improve public health literacy with responsible, understandable explanations.
  • Create room for collaboration and continuous knowledge-sharing.

How learning happens

Flexible formats, grounded in real learning needs.

Programs can be delivered online, offline, or through a hybrid format, depending on the material, audience, and practical requirements.

  • OnlineWebinars, digital learning, articles, videos, and accessible reference materials.
  • OfflineSeminars, workshops, practical training, and direct professional exchange.
  • HybridConnected learning that combines broad access with focused in-person experience.

Near-term priorities
