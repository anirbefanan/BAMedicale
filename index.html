A useful distinction

A tumor is not always cancer.

“Tumor” describes an abnormal mass or growth. Some neoplasms are benign; malignant tumors can invade nearby tissue and may spread to other parts of the body.

Understand tumor vs cancer
01

Benign

Often grows locally and does not invade or spread in the way malignant cancer can.

02

Malignant

Can invade surrounding tissue. Diagnosis depends on clinical and pathology findings.

03

Metastatic

Describes cancer that has spread from its primary site to a distant part of the body.
