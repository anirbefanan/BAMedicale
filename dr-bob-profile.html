Dr. dr. Bob Andinata, Sp.B., Subsp. Onk(K)

Surgical oncologist and head and neck cancer specialist whose work connects clinical care, public health, medical education, and institutional leadership.

His professional journey spans community health service, specialist cancer care, international fellowship training, and ongoing education for both public and professional audiences.

Career journey

A career shaped by care, training, and knowledge-sharing.

From frontline public health to specialist oncology leadership, each stage has informed a practical approach to learning, prevention, diagnosis, and compassionate care.

  1. 2000-2004

    Public-health service

    Worked as a medical doctor and led public health centres in Tabalong, South Kalimantan, and Belakang Padang, Batam.

  2. 2004-2013

    Surgical training

    Completed general surgery and surgical oncology training at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Indonesia.

  3. 2013-present

    Specialist cancer care

    Serves as a surgical oncologist at Dharmais National Cancer Center Hospital alongside continuing specialist clinical practice.

  4. 2018-2019

    International fellowships

    Completed head and neck oncology fellowships at Tokyo National Cancer Centre and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, New York.

  5. 2020-present

    Early detection leadership

    Leads early detection and health-promotion work at Dharmais National Cancer Center Hospital.

  6. 2025-present

    Department leadership

    Serves as Head of Department of Head and Neck at Dharmais National Cancer Center Hospital.
