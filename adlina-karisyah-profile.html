Medical profile & professional journey

dr. Adlina Karisyah, SpB.

Surgical training shaped by clinical service, research, and education.

dr. Adlina completed surgical specialist education at Universitas Indonesia in 2025. Her experience spans community and hospital internships, general medical practice, oncology clinical assistance, research, and teaching.

Alongside clinical development, she has contributed to patient information, scientific activities, medical relief, and student organizations. She is part of BA Medicale's Medical & Professional Staff.

Medical & clinical journey

From medical foundations to specialist surgery.

Her path connects research, frontline service, and surgical training. Several clinical, teaching, and research appointments ran alongside one another; their dates are retained below.

  1. 2011-2016

    Medical education

    Universitas Indonesia

    Undertook medical education while participating in academic peer learning, community service, and medical-student organizations. Earned a cum laude distinction in the professional program in 2017.

  2. Jul 2015 - Aug 2016

    Early research assistance

    Assisted with research and thesis writing on digestive and pediatric surgery topics, developing experience in scientific work alongside her medical education.

  3. Sep 2016 - Jan 2017

    Oncology department internship

    Rumah Sakit Kanker Dharmais

    Collected research-subject data, wrote scientific articles for the surgical oncology department's website, and assisted with a cancer patient guide. Helped organize a cancer symposium for nurses and the public, attended scientific meetings and complex case discussions, and observed operations.

  4. Feb 2017 - Feb 2018

    Community & hospital internships

    Tidore Kepulauan, North Maluku

    From February to June 2017, served at Puskesmas Soasio and Puskesmas Tosa, providing outpatient care and taking part in community health-post activities and research.

    From June 2017 to February 2018, worked at Rumah Sakit Daerah Tidore Kepulauan in emergency, inpatient, ICU, NICU, and outpatient services. Observed and assisted operations, organized a symposium for general practitioners, and prepared outpatient health-promotion materials.

  5. Mar 2018 - Jun 2020

    Oncology research assistance

    RSCM-FKUI, Surgical Oncology Division

    Worked as a research assistant to Dr. dr. Diani Kartini, SpB(K)Onk at Rumah Sakit Cipto Mangunkusumo-FKUI, assisting with chemotherapy-module preparation and research-paper writing.

  6. Mar - Jun 2018

    Teaching for medical examinations

    Optima Preparation

    Tutored material for UKMPPD preparation, supporting doctors preparing for the medical competency examination.

  7. Apr 2018 - Jun 2020

    Clinical assistance in surgical oncology

    Rumah Sakit Kanker Dharmais

    Worked with outpatient and inpatient surgical oncology services, observed and assisted operations, and attended complex oncology case discussions. Assisted research and the writing of revisions to the PERABOI protocol.

  8. Sep 2018 - Jun 2020

    General medical practice

    Served part-time as a general practitioner at Rumah Sakit Yadika Pondok Bambu from September 2018 to August 2019, and at Rumah Sakit Kanker Dharmais from January 2019 to June 2020.

  9. 2020-2025

    Surgical specialist education

    Universitas Indonesia

    Completed the surgical specialist program, supported by an LPDP specialist-doctor scholarship awarded in 2019. Her 2025 distinctions include second-ranked graduate in the national competency examination of Kolegium Bedah Indonesia.

    During this period, she continued professional development in surgical skills, trauma, endoscopy, laparoscopy, burns, perioperative care, and wound and stoma care. In 2025, she also took part in medical service in Dawai, Papua, with RSA Nusa Waluya II.
